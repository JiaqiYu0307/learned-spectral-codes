02 / THE IDEA

Keep spectral information.
Keep the RGB renderer.

Three RGB channels describe a colour, but not the spectrum behind it. When light interacts with glass, metals or scattering media, collapsing that spectrum too early changes the final appearance.

We encode visible-range spectra as compact latent vectors that a conventional RGB renderer can process. Two passes carry six latent channels through the existing pipeline; a linear decoder then reconstructs the spectral result.

One codec covers reflectance, illumination, coloured glass, conductors and participating media. Lightweight upsamplers also provide a route for legacy RGB assets.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Change the representation.
Preserve the arithmetic.

Select a stage to follow the spectral signal through an RGB pipeline.

z = Wenc s

Encode what light transport needs

A non-negative linear encoder compresses each spectrum to six channels. Linearity preserves scaling and addition exactly; training makes channel-wise multiplication approximate the corresponding spectral operation.

WHY k = 6?

Three channels face a trade-off between colour reconstruction and multiplication. Nine channels require another render pass with limited quality benefit in the paper's evaluation. Six channels are the practical operating point.

05 / NARROWBAND ILLUMINATION

Not only broadband.
True narrowband lights.

Three Gaussian illumination cells test the codec under blue, green and red narrowband light. Each has a full width at half maximum (FWHM) of 40 nm.

Within each cell, RGB, our k = 6 result and the spectral reference use one common exposure. Errors are ΔE₂₀₀₀ against the spectral reference over the 52-cell Cornell-box sweep.

06 / CROSS-RENDERER EVALUATION

Four renderers.
The same two-pass interface.

Compare each engine's codec render with its RGB baseline and reconstructed spectral reference. You can also select the full route that starts from RGB assets.

The final paper constructs a 16-pass spectral reference inside each production pipeline using uplifted material spectra and known light spectra. “Codec” applies the six-channel codec to those spectral assets; “Full RGB-asset route” adds the paper's RGB upsamplers. It is a constructed reference, not a native spectral mode of these engines. The selected Maya scene uses structured lighting; the paper also reports a nearly flat broadband case where RGB is most accurate.

Where it applies

Spectral effects expressible through channel-wise light-transport arithmetic, including reflectance, illumination, coloured glass, conductors, subsurface scattering and participating media.
